### Context and understanding of the domain

Here are some key concepts used throughout this project:

#### Key concepts

- **Artificial intelligence (AI) model**: A computer system designed to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence, such as natural language understanding, image recognition, or decision-making.
- **Optimization algorithm**: A method used to adjust the parameters of an AI model in order to improve its performance on a given task.
- **Reinforcement learning (RL)**: A learning paradigm where an agent learns to make decisions by interacting with an environment, guided by a reward signal. What is learned is a decision-making policy that maximizes long-term cumulative reward.
- **On-policy vs off-policy algorithm**: In reinforcement learning, on-policy algorithms learn from actions taken by the current policy, while off-policy algorithms can learn from data generated by a different policy.
- **Fine-tuning**: The process of adapting a pre-trained model to a specific task by continuing its training on relevant data.
- **Vision-language-action (VLA) models**: Large models capable of understanding multiple types of information (such as images and text), and then generating motor actions for robotics applications.

#### RL algorithms

Here is a non-exhaustive list of reinforcement learning algorithms relevant to this project:

- **PPO (Proximal Policy Optimization)**: An on-policy policy gradient algorithm that stabilizes learning by constraining policy updates through a clipping function, limiting overly large changes between iterations.
- **SAC (Soft Actor-Critic)**: An off-policy actor-critic algorithm that jointly optimizes expected reward and policy entropy, promoting a balance between performance and exploration.
- **GRPO (Group Relative Policy Optimization)**: A recent policy optimization variant used notably in the context of language models, which estimates advantages relatively within a group of samples, reducing or removing the need for an explicit critic in some formulations.

#### VLA models

Here are some vision-language-action (VLA) models relevant to this project:

- **OpenVLA**: An open-source vision-language-action model designed to perform robotic tasks from natural language instructions and multimodal visual inputs.
- **π0 (Pi0)**: A foundation model for robotics developed in the context of recent VLA research, aiming to learn generalizable robotic policies from multimodal data.
- **GR00T**: A foundation model for humanoid robotics developed by NVIDIA, designed to support learning generalized robotic behaviors from visual and linguistic inputs.

#### Testing environments

Here are some simulation environments used for training and evaluating RL models in the context of robotics:

- **LIBERO**: A robotics benchmark designed to evaluate the generalization and transfer capabilities of robotic policies across varied and compositional tasks.
- **RoboCasa**: A simulation environment focused on realistic household tasks, enabling evaluation of manipulation and interaction with everyday objects.
- **ManiSkill**: A robotics simulation framework oriented toward manipulation tasks, offering complex environments that require precision and planning.
